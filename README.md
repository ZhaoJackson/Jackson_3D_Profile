## About Me

I'm **Jackson Zhao**, a data scientist and AI researcher working at the intersection of neuroscience, mental health, and artificial intelligence. I hold a Master of Arts in Research Methodology & Quantitative Methods from Columbia University and have worked across institutions including Mount Sinai, NYU Langone Health, Columbia University, and the United Nations.

My research focuses on passive Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) and affective computing — using brain signals, behavioral data, and AI models to better understand mental states such as attention, stress, emotion, and cognitive load. I've built machine-driven benchmarks to evaluate suicide-prevention chatbots, developed EEG and synthetic neural data pipelines, and created LLM systems for high-stakes mental health applications.
